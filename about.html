About Basketwise

Basketwise helps U.S. households understand the real cost of everyday purchases.

Editorial approach

We explain the math behind prices and promotions, distinguish a lower unit price from a lower checkout total, and remind readers to check local terms. Examples in our guides are illustrative, not current retail offers. Current promotions, when added, should include a source link, location, date checked, expiration, and eligibility conditions.

Advertising and affiliate links

There are currently no ads or affiliate links on this site. If we add affiliate recommendations, we will identify paid links clearly near the recommendation. Commissions will not change the calculation or stated limitations of a deal.

Corrections

Prices and promotion rules change. Check the retailer's current listing and checkout before purchase. If you find an error, use the contact link on the home page.
