Build a grocery list around your budget

A sale is useful only when it helps you buy meals you will eat within the money you have set aside.

Start at home

Check the refrigerator, freezer, and pantry. Write down the foods that need to be used first. Plan meals around those ingredients and your household's schedule before looking at store promotions.

Set a realistic limit

Choose a spending limit for the trip and leave a little room for price differences or a missing staple. Make a list of essentials first. Add optional items only after estimating the essentials' total.

Compare the whole basket

A store with a very cheap featured item may still have a higher total for your full list. Compare the cost of the basket, travel, delivery, and any membership fees. Prices and availability can vary by location.

A simple order: Use what you have → plan meals → list needed ingredients → estimate the total → check relevant offers → revise the list.

If an offer requires buying extra items, compare the promotion checkout total with your original list before deciding.
